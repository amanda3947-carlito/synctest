Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

aux is a dating app where people get to know each other by building things together: a mixtape, a plan, a dinner. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. aux is for people 18 and over.

What we collect

  • Your phone number. It is how you sign in. We send a one-time code to it by text message. It is never shown on your profile.
  • What you put on your profile. Your first name (and last name, which is never shown), birthday (we show your age, not the date), photos and videos, gender, what you are looking for, the details you choose to add (such as hometown, work, education, height and habits), your answers to prompts including any voice notes, your badge, cover, vibes, the music in your rotation, the shows, films and books you list, and the spots you add.
  • What you build and send. The mixtapes, plans and other builds you make with people, the changes you each make, notes you send with them, and messages after a match.
  • Who you pass on, block or report, so we can keep them out of your feed and look into reports.
  • Your contacts, only if you choose to connect them. We turn each number into a one-way code on your phone before anything leaves it, and use those codes only to show mutual connections. We never store your contacts' names or numbers.
  • Your location, only while you search for a spot and only if you allow it. It is used to show places near you first. We do not store it or track where you go.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your profile to other people on aux, show you people, and carry builds and messages between you.
  • To keep aux safe: review reports, remove people who break the rules, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To fix problems and make the app better.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

People on aux see your profile: your first name, age, photos and videos, and what you chose to add. They never see your phone number, last name, exact birthday or contacts. What you build with someone is visible to the two of you. Anyone you block can no longer see you, and you cannot see them.

Services that help us run aux

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • An SMS provider delivers your sign-in codes.
  • Apple's iTunes Search helps you find songs, shows, films and books; OpenStreetMap (Photon) helps you find spots; Wikimedia Commons supplies photos of public places. These receive only what you type into a search, or a place's location.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, builds and messages are deleted. Copies in backups are removed on our normal backup cycle. Reports you made, or that were made about you, may be kept where we need them to keep aux safe.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove anything on your profile at any time from your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: open your profile, tap the “…” menu, then Delete account. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Turn off location or contacts in your iPhone's Settings at any time.
  • To ask for a copy of your information, or anything else about your privacy, contact us below.

Children

aux is only for people 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
