Support

aux is a dating app where you get to know people by building things together. Here are answers to the questions people ask most.

Signing in

Enter your phone number and we will text you a six-digit code. If it does not arrive within a minute, check the number and tap back to try again.

Changing your profile

Tap your photo at the top left of the feed. Edit shows everything you can change; View shows your profile the way other people see it.

Builds

Open someone's profile and tap “Make it a mixtape” to remix their rotation, then send it. They add to it and send it back. When you both agree, it is a match. Everything in progress lives on the Builds tab.

Staying safe

On anyone's profile, tap the “…” button to report or block them. Reports are anonymous: they will not know it was you. Blocking someone ends anything you were building together, and neither of you will see the other again.

Deleting your account

Open your profile, tap the “…” menu, then Delete account. Your profile, photos, builds and messages are deleted right away. This cannot be undone.
